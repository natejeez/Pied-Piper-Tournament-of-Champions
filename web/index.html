2026 Rules

The public view stays song-first. The field contains 72 songs, with eight play-ins reducing the tournament to a 64-song single-elimination bracket.

Blind submissionsParticipant ownership remains hidden from matchup cards.
Head-to-headEvery matchup advances one song and eliminates the other.
Protected seedingSongs from the same participant cannot meet in the first main-bracket round.
Auditable votingEach submitted vote is attached to one participant, one matchup, and one song.

Stats

Results stay hidden while voting is open. Participant-linked vote records are retained so trend and accuracy statistics can be calculated after the appropriate reveal point.

About HMPP '26

Harry Men Pied Piper is a blind tournament of songs. The site keeps matchup cards focused on the music while authenticated participant identity is used privately for vote integrity and later statistics.
