2026 Rules

The public view stays song-first. The field contains 72 songs, with eight play-ins reducing the tournament to a 64-song single-elimination bracket.

Blind submissionsParticipant ownership remains hidden from the public matchup view.
Duplicate controlOnly one copy of the same song/composition may enter unless the recordings are substantially different.
Protected seedingSongs from the same participant cannot face each other in the first main-bracket round.
Head-to-headEvery matchup advances one song and permanently eliminates the other.
Eight play-insSixteen songs contest eight spots before the 64-song bracket begins.
Auditable resultsMatch IDs and advancement remain reconstructable from the tournament record.

About HMPP '26

Harry Men Pied Piper is a blind tournament of songs. The site is intentionally designed around the music rather than the people who submitted it. Listening, voting, results, rationale reveals, participant accounts, and historical statistics can be layered onto the same song-first foundation.

Public bracket prototype. Participant identities remain intentionally excluded. The cards display song IDs, artist, and title; listening, voting, rationale, and results can be layered in later without changing the bracket's public identity model.
